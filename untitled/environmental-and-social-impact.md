# Environmental & Social Impact

Bottle Up will measure impact using operational metrics.

#### Environmental

* kilograms of plastic recovered
* tonnes recovered
* number of bottles collected
* material diverted toward recycling
* repeat collection rate

#### Economic

* rewards distributed
* collectors engaged
* recycling partners supplied
* businesses served
* paid collection opportunities created

#### Community

* active collection locations
* households participating
* schools or organisations participating
* community collection campaigns

Impact claims will be based on recorded activity rather than broad estimates.
