# Business & Revenue Model

Bottle Up has two sides.

**Consumer side:** users supply recyclable materials.

**Business side:** recyclers and businesses create revenue opportunities.

#### Revenue flow

**Material aggregation → recycling partner → material sales**

**Business recovery program → collection/reporting service → business fee**

**Verified recovery data → reporting → business service**

### Revenue stream 1: Material aggregation

Bottle Up can earn through the sale of aggregated recyclable material to recycling partners.

The margin depends on:

**sale price − collection cost − logistics − sorting/handling − rewards − operating costs**

### Revenue stream 2: Business recovery programs

Businesses that produce or use significant quantities of plastic can work with Bottle Up to support collection and recovery.

Potential services include collection campaigns, employee/customer recycling programs, community recovery programs and recovery reporting.

### Revenue stream 3: Recovery data and reporting

As collection volume increases, Bottle Up can provide businesses with verified records of kilograms recovered, material types, collection locations, dates and recycling partner destination.
