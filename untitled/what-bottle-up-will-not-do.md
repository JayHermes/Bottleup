# What Bottle Up Will Not Do

Bottle Up will not attempt to become:

* a general waste-management company overnight
* a plastic manufacturer
* a recycling plant in every city
* a replacement for existing recyclers
* a platform that accepts every type of waste immediately

The initial focus is narrower:

> **Collect recyclable plastic, verify it, reward participation and move the material to credible recycling channels.**

That focus allows the business to learn the economics before expanding its scope.
