# Executive Summary

Plastic recycling depends on one thing before anything else: getting usable plastic waste into the recycling value chain.

The global plastics industry produces hundreds of millions of tonnes of plastic every year. In 2019, global plastic production reached approximately 460 million tonnes, while plastic waste reached 353 million tonnes. Only 9% of plastic waste was ultimately recycled. [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/global-plastics-outlook_de747aef-en/full-report/component-6.html)

Nigeria faces the same structural problem at a different scale.

Plastic is widely used across food packaging, bottled and sachet water, household products, retail and manufacturing. Yet collection, sorting, transportation and reliable access to recycling markets remain fragmented.

Bottle Up sits between the point where plastic becomes waste and the businesses that can recover its value.

The model is simple:

**People collect plastic → Bottle Up coordinates pickup and verification → material moves to recycling partners → users receive rewards → businesses can support and track recovery.**

Bottle Up launched its first pilot on **28 September 2026** in **Ikot Ekpene, Uyo and Eket**.

The first 24-hour snapshot recorded:

* **8 users signed up**
* **7 pickup requests**
* **3 launch locations**

These figures are early traction, not a final pilot result. The pilot is intended to test demand, pickup logistics, material verification, reward mechanics and recycler relationships before expansion.
