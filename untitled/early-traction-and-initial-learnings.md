# Early Traction & Initial Learnings

Within the first 24 hours after launch:

* **8 users signed up**
* **7 pickup requests were submitted**
* **3 locations were active**

This is an early snapshot, not a final pilot result.

The next phase of measurement should focus on:

#### Acquisition

* registrations
* cost per acquired user
* referral rate

#### Usage

* pickup requests
* completed pickups
* repeat requests
* active users

#### Material

* kilograms collected
* kilograms per pickup
* material type
* rejection rate

#### Economics

* cost per pickup
* logistics cost/kg
* reward cost/kg
* revenue/kg
* gross margin/kg

#### Partnerships

* active recycling partners
* business partners
* material purchase agreements
* recurring collection programs
