# Competitive Landscape

Bottle Up operates alongside several existing models.

| Model                        | Primary focus           | Bottle Up's difference                   |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Informal collectors          | Material collection     | Digital access + user-facing collection  |
| Recycling companies          | Processing/recycling    | Focus begins earlier in the chain        |
| Waste-management companies   | General waste           | Plastic-focused recovery                 |
| Collection centres           | Aggregation             | Mobile/community access                  |
| Recycling reward platforms   | Incentives              | Collection + rewards + business recovery |
| Corporate recycling programs | Brand-specific recovery | Open platform approach                   |

Bottle Up is not trying to replace recycling companies.

Its role is to create a more reliable flow of material into them.
