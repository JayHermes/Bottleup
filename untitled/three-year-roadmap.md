# Three-Year Roadmap

### 2026: Prove the model

Focus: Ikot Ekpene, Uyo and Eket.

Priorities:

* complete the initial pilot
* improve pickup operations
* validate reward mechanics
* establish reliable recycling partners
* measure unit economics
* improve material verification
* document repeat-user behaviour
* sign first business recovery partnerships

**Main question:** Can Bottle Up collect plastic repeatedly at a cost that makes economic sense?

### 2027: Expand the network

Priorities:

* expand beyond the initial Akwa Ibom locations
* increase collection density
* build a larger collector network
* onboard more recycling partners
* launch business recovery programs
* improve reporting
* establish repeatable city-launch playbooks

**Main question:** Can the model be repeated in another Nigerian city without rebuilding the operation from scratch?

### 2028: Build a national recovery network

Priorities:

* expand into additional Nigerian markets
* connect larger recycling buyers
* support corporate recovery programs
* increase recovered material volume
* strengthen collection infrastructure
* develop regional partnerships

The long-term objective is to build a network through which plastic can move from consumers and businesses back into productive use.
