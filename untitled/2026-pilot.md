# 2026 Pilot

**Launch date:** 28 September 2026

**Initial locations:** Ikot Ekpene, Uyo and Eket

#### Pilot purpose

The first pilot is designed to answer practical questions:

1. Will people consistently request plastic pickups?
2. What materials are most commonly collected?
3. How much material can be recovered per pickup?
4. What does each pickup actually cost?
5. How frequently do users return?
6. How should rewards be structured?
7. What quality standards do recycling partners require?
8. Which collection routes make economic sense?
9. How much material can Bottle Up aggregate from each location?
10. Which business partnerships can support the model?

The pilot is an operating test, not just a marketing exercise.
