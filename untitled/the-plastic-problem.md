# The Plastic Problem

Plastic itself is not the problem.

Plastic is useful because it is lightweight, durable, cheap to produce and suitable for many applications.

The problem begins when a product reaches the end of its useful life and there is no efficient route for it to return to the production cycle.

A plastic bottle can therefore follow two very different paths.

**Path A: Lost value**

Consumer → discarded → drain/street/dumpsite → environmental leakage

**Path B: Recovered value**

Consumer → collection → sorting → recycling → recycled material → new product

The difference between these paths is largely a collection problem.

The OECD estimates that nearly two-thirds of global plastic waste comes from applications with lifespans of less than five years, with packaging alone accounting for 40%. [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/global-plastics-outlook_de747aef-en/full-report/component-6.html)
