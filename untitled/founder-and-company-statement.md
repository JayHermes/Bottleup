# Founder & Company Statement

Bottle Up started with a simple observation: plastic waste already has value, but that value is often lost because there is no easy way for ordinary people to participate in the collection process.

A bottle thrown into a drain is waste. The same bottle collected, verified, sorted and delivered to a recycler becomes a raw material.

Bottle Up is being built around that difference.

Our goal is to make it easier for people to return plastic waste, easier for collectors and recyclers to access usable materials, and easier for businesses to track and support the recovery of the plastic they put into the market.

We are starting in Nigeria, beginning with communities where we can directly test the model, improve collection operations and build relationships with recycling partners.

Bottle Up is not trying to solve every part of waste management at once. We are starting with one clear problem:

> **How do we get more plastic from where it becomes waste to where it can be used again?**
