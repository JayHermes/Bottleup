# Technology & Operations

Bottle Up's technology is designed around four basic functions.

#### User management

Accounts, profiles, pickup history and reward balances.

#### Collection management

Pickup requests, locations, assignments and status.

#### Verification

Weight, material type, quality and verification records.

#### Reporting

Collection volumes, user activity, partner activity and recovery records.

The technology should remain simple for the average user. The product should feel like a recycling service rather than a complicated technology platform.
