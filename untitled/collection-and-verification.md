# Collection & Verification

Collection is one of the most important parts of the Bottle Up model.

Simply collecting large quantities of plastic is not enough. The material must be useful to the next participant in the chain.

Bottle Up intends to record:

* user
* collection location
* material category
* weight
* collection date
* verification status
* destination
* recycler/partner
* reward issued

Over time, this creates a record of material movement rather than simply a record of users.

#### Initial material focus

Bottle Up can begin with commonly traded recyclable materials such as:

* PET bottles
* PP
* PE
* HDPE
* LDPE
* other materials accepted by confirmed recycling partners

The exact accepted-material list will depend on partner requirements and local economics.
