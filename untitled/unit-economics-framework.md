# Unit Economics Framework

Bottle Up should measure its economics per kilogram rather than only counting users.

#### Revenue per kg

**Material sale revenue/kg + business recovery revenue/kg**

#### Variable cost per kg

**Collection + transport + sorting + verification + rewards + payment costs**

#### Contribution margin

**Revenue/kg − variable cost/kg**

For each pickup:

**Pickup contribution = material revenue + service revenue − pickup cost − rewards − handling cost**

The key question is:

> **Does collecting one more kilogram of plastic create or destroy value?**

The numbers should be measured during the 2026 pilot rather than invented in advance.
