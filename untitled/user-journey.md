# User Journey

#### 1. Collect

A user collects recyclable plastic from their home, workplace, school, business or community.

#### 2. Request

The user opens Bottle Up and requests a pickup.

#### 3. Pickup

Bottle Up or an assigned collection partner picks up the material. Pickup is intended to be part of the normal Bottle Up service.

#### 4. Verify

The material is checked for weight, material type, contamination and general quality.

#### 5. Points

Verified material earns points according to Bottle Up's reward rules.

#### 6. Redeem

Users can redeem eligible rewards through available options.

#### 7. Recovery

The material is aggregated and transferred to an appropriate recycling partner.
