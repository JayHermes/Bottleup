# Closing

Bottle Up is built around a simple idea:

> **Waste does not have to remain waste.**

A bottle has little value when it is lying in a drain.

It has value when someone collects it, sorts it, verifies it and gets it to the right recycling partner.

The opportunity for Bottle Up is to make that process easier, more reliable and more rewarding for everyone involved.

The 2026 pilot is the beginning.

The immediate goal is not to claim that the problem has been solved.

It is to prove that a better collection system can work — one pickup, one kilogram and one community at a time.
