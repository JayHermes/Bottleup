# The Gap Bottle Up Is Addressing

#### For individuals

People may have recyclable plastic but do not know where to take it, do not have time to transport it, do not know whether it has value, or receive little immediate benefit from separating it.

#### For collectors

Collectors need reliable sources of material, predictable pickup routes, better access to households and businesses, and information about where material is available.

#### For recyclers

Recyclers need consistent material supply, appropriate material types, acceptable quality and predictable volumes.

#### For businesses

Companies increasingly need to understand what happens to packaging and plastic materials connected to their operations. Collection data, however, can be fragmented.

Bottle Up is designed around this gap.
